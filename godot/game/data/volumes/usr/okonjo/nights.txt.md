MY FATHER SAT IN THIS CHAIR FOR FORTY YEARS AND HIS
MOTHER BEFORE HIM, AND AS FAR AS ANYONE CAN TELL THE
VOICE HAS SAID THE SAME THING EVERY SINGLE NIGHT OF IT.
ONE BEAT, ONE ACK, THE LAMP CHAIN, SLEEP.

THE COUNCIL CALLS IT THE HEARING AND SENDS A NEW PRAYER
CARD EVERY WEEK, AND THE NUMBERS ON IT ARE ALWAYS A LITTLE
WRONG, SEEMINGLY MORE WRONG EVERY YEAR. NOBODY UP THERE
WANTS TO HEAR THAT. I STOPPED TELLING THEM.

THE LEFT WHEEL STICKS. TAP THE NEEDLE GLASS IF IT HANGS.
TURN SLOW, LISTEN FOR THE TONE, NOT THE NEEDLE.

SOME NIGHTS I THINK THE VOICE IS WAITING FOR SOMETHING.
THEN THE BELL RINGS AND I GO TO BED.
