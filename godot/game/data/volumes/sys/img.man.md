IMG DECODER V2.1, OPERATOR MANUAL
(C) SOWING PROGRAMME

AN IMG ITEM ARRIVES AS ONE LONG LINE OF SAMPLES. THE MONITOR
CUTS IT INTO ROWS. DECODE <ID> PUTS A STORED ITEM ON THE
MONITOR FOR TUNING.

LINE      THE LENGTH OF ONE ROW. WRONG, THE PICTURE LEANS
          INTO STRIPES. RIGHT, IT STANDS UP STRAIGHT.
OFFSET    WHERE A ROW BEGINS. TURN UNTIL THE PICTURE IS
          WHOLE AND NOT SPLIT ACROSS THE SCREEN.
CONTRAST  TO TASTE.
COMMIT    KEEPS THE PICTURE AS TUNED. A DECODED ITEM TAKES
          ONE UNIT OF STORAGE. SHOW <ID> BRINGS IT BACK.

A CAL FRAME SHOWS UPRIGHT BARS WHEN LINE IS RIGHT. TUNE ONE
FIRST TO LEARN THE KNOB.

BLACK ROWS WERE LOST IN TRANSFER. SNOWY ROWS CAME DOWN
NOISY.
