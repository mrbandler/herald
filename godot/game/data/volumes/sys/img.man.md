IMG DECODER V2.1, OPERATOR MANUAL
(C) SOWING PROGRAMME

AN IMG ITEM ARRIVES AS ONE LONG LINE OF SAMPLES. THE MONITOR
CUTS IT INTO ROWS. DECODE <ID> PUTS A STORED ITEM ON THE
MONITOR FOR TUNING.

LINE      THE LENGTH OF ONE ROW. WRONG, THE PICTURE LEANS
          INTO STRIPES. RIGHT, IT STANDS UP STRAIGHT.
OFFSET    WHERE A ROW BEGINS. TURN UNTIL THE PICTURE IS
          WHOLE AND NOT SPLIT ACROSS THE SCREEN.
CONTRAST  TO TASTE.
COMMIT    KEEPS THE PICTURE AS TUNED. A DECODED ITEM TAKES
          ONE UNIT OF STORAGE. SHOW <ID> BRINGS IT BACK.

HERALD CARRIES TWO CAMERAS, WIDE AND NARROW, AND EACH SENDS
ROWS OF ITS OWN LENGTH. A PASS WITH PICTURES ALSO SENDS ONE
CAL FRAME PER CAMERA, FOR ALL OF THAT CAMERA'S PICTURES.

DECODE <ID> CAL <ID> SETS THE KNOBS FROM A STORED CAL. THE
DECODER IS OLD: IT SETS THEM CLOSE, NOT EXACT. FINISH BY
HAND OR COMMIT AS IT IS. A CAL FROM THE OTHER CAMERA SETS
THEM WRONG. A CAL COSTS SPACE; WITHOUT ONE, FIND LINE ON THE
PICTURE ITSELF. ITS BARS STAND UPRIGHT WHEN LINE IS RIGHT.
