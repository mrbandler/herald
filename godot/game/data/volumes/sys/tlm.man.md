SPC       THE SPECTROMETER, ON OR OFF.
AMP       THE TRANSMITTER AMPLIFIER.
BANK      THE ACTIVE MEMORY BANK.
HEATERS   INSTRUMENT HEATERS. OFF MEANS DAMAGE.
POSITION  WHERE HERALD IS, BY CATALOG NAME.
POINTING  THE CORRECTION FOR THE NEXT PASS. ADD IT
          TO THE AZ AND EL ON TONIGHT'S PRAYER.
STATUS    THE PHASE HERALD IS IN AND WHAT IT AWAITS.
