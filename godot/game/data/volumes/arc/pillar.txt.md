SOWING PROGRAMME. ARK-1, OPERATING NOTE.

ARK-1 STANDS IN THE LAUNCH SHAFT OF LISTENING POST 4.
IT IS FUELLED AND SEALED. IT WAITS FOR ONE SIGNAL.

IF HERALD'S DESCENT CONFIRMS TGT-A, THE STATION
SENDS THE WORD AND ARK-1 OPENS TO THE WELL.

IF THE SURVEY IS INCOMPLETE, THE STATION MAY SEND
HERALD ON TO TGT-B AND WAIT. THE WELL WAITS WITH IT.

DO NOT LET THEM CALL IT A PILLAR.
