To whoever sits in this chair after me,

the Listening is simple, and it always was. When the clock nears the hour, Seek: set the brass wheels to the AZ and EL on tonight's prayer and turn them slowly until the Voice rises out of the hiss. Bind: turn the black dial to the card's DIAL until the green lamp holds. Then wait, the screen will tell you when it listens. Speak: lean to the screen, type SEQ ADD ACK, and when the Speak lamp lights, type TX and hold the Voice until it is sent. Pull the lamp chain after and rest.

Nothing ever changes. It's only the heartbeat.

O.
