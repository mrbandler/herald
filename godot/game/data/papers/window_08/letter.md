Listener,

the Voice is with the Daughter. It will not wait much longer, and it will ask us one last thing. Tonight it sends the key for the verdict. UNLOCK VERDICT with it.

There are two words. DESCEND, and the Voice falls into her air and tells us the truth on its way down; the Pillar opens on what it sends. TRANSFER, and the Voice leaves for Tarba, and the answer waits for whoever sits in your chair after you.

Before you choose, look at what you kept. Her face, sharp and set straight. The taste of her air. If you hold both, you know what I know.

Send one word with your ACK. Or send none, and the Pillar stays shut.

I will not write again.
